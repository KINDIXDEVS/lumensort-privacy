Lumen Sort Privacy Policy

Publisher: KINDIX DEVS
Contact: kindixdevs@gmail.com
Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what Lumen Sort ("the app") does with information when you play.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, boosters, settings, badges and purchases status are stored locally on your device. We do not run accounts and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

2. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and other technical information to show ads and measure performance. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Purchases

Purchases (Premium subscription, coin packs and the ad-free pack) are processed by Google Play. Subscription status is verified through RevenueCat. We do not see or store your payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local daily reminders on your device. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Play and RevenueCat) as needed to run ads and purchases.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset all progress" in Settings removes it. For questions or requests about data held by our providers, contact us at the address above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
